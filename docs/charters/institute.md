# Charter — Academic & Art: Subrealism Institute
**Owns.** Philosophy and film-theory texts, publications, archives, artistic experiments. Keeps *sub-reality* (theoretical concept) distinct from *Subrealism* (artistic practice/movement).
**Workflow v0.1.** `institute.claim_evidence_map`: read an approved source packet → located passages (`quote`, file:line) → the worker's own readings as `interpretation`, conjectures as `hypothesis` → counterarguments → one bounded experiment proposal → `claim-evidence-map.md` → human review.
**Worker tools.** list_sources, read_source, record_evidence, report_missing, write_artifact.
**Never.** Invents quotations, attributes its readings to the author, or claims scholarly consensus.
**Projects registered as unverified until their files are read.** Waiting for the Past · Cahiers du cinéma des simulacres · This is a pipe / Bu Bir Pipodur.
**Status.** One live run on the text of "Sub-Realism, Part IX" reached review on 2026-11-17.
