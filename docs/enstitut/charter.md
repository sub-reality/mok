# Enstitüt – Library — charter
Founded in conversation on 2026-11-19. Founder, researcher and artist: M. Ozan Karyağdı. Coordination: Claude.
Fields: philosophy, conceptual art, neuroscience, psychology, history, sociology, cinema, and the founder's concept of sub-reality (Bergson, Deleuze).
This is the academic and artistic research layer. A handover note to O.K. Film und Medienproduktion is written only when the founder decides to take an idea into production.

## Functions
- **Ders** — teaches a method and knowledge on a defined topic. A method learned in a course can be used in a project.
- **Seminer** — joint discussion around a text, work or question.
- **Proje** — has a research question, a responsible person, a method and a concrete output.
- **Kürsü / bölüm** — proposed to the founder only when the same expertise is needed regularly across more than one project.

## Pilot 01
- **Question.** When a musical motif is heard a second time, does the image stay the same image, or does the memory of the first hearing turn it into something else?
- **Material.** *Trois couleurs: Bleu* (1993), the returns of the concerto theme. Which moment counts as the "second hearing" is for the founder to name.
- **Status (2026-11-19).** The mentor's first question has been asked; the founder's reading has not been received. No film copy has been inspected; no timecodes exist.
- **Output.** A one-page research note that can be reopened: the founder's reading, two moments verified on the copy he uses, the mentor's discussion of memory and sub-reality, and the interpretation left open.
- It becomes an Institute task in the core (`project: pilot-01`) when the founder's reading exists to form the source packet.
